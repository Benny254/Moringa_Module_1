Adventures in Fire Island

Trip Summary

My recent trip to Fire Island was an unforgettable adventure filled with beautiful beaches, scenic trails, and peaceful coastal views. I spent time exploring the lighthouse, walking along the sandy shoreline, and enjoying the fresh ocean breeze. The natural beauty and relaxing atmosphere made it the perfect getaway from busy city life.
